Training Philosophy

Personalise Your Own Training Program

Just starting out, returning from injury, or fitting training around a busy life? The VIMP — Volume Intensity Management Plan — is a four-step system for easing into full training without overload or injury.

December 20, 2023 5 min read By Mick Di Betta

Not everyone begins a training plan at the same starting point. You might be just starting out and can't manage the full program workload. You might be returning from injury or a long break. You might want to add extra training phases into your season. All of these are valid — and all require personalising the plan to fit where you actually are, not where you wish you were.

The best plans are the ones that can flex with your everyday lifestyle while still maintaining the firm guidelines of progressive overload and ticking off minor goals along the way. Those minor goals — the motivational steps — are what carry you to the major goal at the end of the season.

The VIMP: Volume Intensity Management Plan

The VIMP is a four-step system that sets volume and intensity in easy-to-manage progressive stages. The goal is to build adaptation at each step before moving to the next, without overloading or risking injury along the way.

Step 1 — Reduce Volume and Intensity (2–4 weeks)

  • Complete sessions 1, 2, and 4 each week (skip session 3)
  • Eliminate any TZ5 work
  • Replace TZ4 with TZ3

Step 2 — Reduce Intensity, Add Volume (2–4 weeks)

  • Complete all four sessions each week
  • Still eliminate TZ5
  • Still replace TZ4 with TZ3

Step 3 — Reduce Volume, Add Intensity (2–4 weeks)

  • Complete all four sessions, but reduce repeats by approximately 20% (e.g. if the program says 5 repeats, do 4; if it says 10, do 8)
  • Reintroduce all five training zones

Step 4 — Full Program

Complete all sessions, all repeats, all training zones as prescribed. By this point your body has adapted to the load progressively and is ready to respond fully.

The key principle: The real success of any training program is the balance between volume and intensity — every interval, every session, every week and month. Take baby steps. Walk before you run. This is the path to a successful and injury-free season.

Whatever your starting point, there is a version of the program that meets you there and builds from it. The VIMP ensures that adaptation happens at every stage — so that when you reach full program load, your body is genuinely prepared, not just surviving it.

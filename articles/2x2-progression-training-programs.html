Interval Training

2×2 Progression Training Programs

Two distinctly different progressive interval sessions that increase training adaptation twice as fast. The science of muscle fibre recruitment and how to build sprint endurance alongside aerobic capacity.

December 20, 2023 8 min read By Mick Di Betta

Skeletal muscles are composed of a mix of fibre types: Type I (slow-twitch), more efficient and fatigue-resistant; and Type IIa and IIx (fast-twitch), larger and more powerful but quicker to fatigue. Muscle fibres are recruited in order — Type I first, then IIa, then IIx — so to recruit and train fast-twitch fibres, you must train at higher intensities. Otherwise the slow-twitch fibres handle the work alone.

Paddlers depend on slow-twitch fibres for endurance. But as exercise physiology makes clear: a trained muscle fibre is a more aerobic muscle fibre. When trained, Type IIa fibres become more fatigue-resistant — and because they produce greater contractile force than Type I, trained IIa fibres drive top-end endurance performance across all paddling distances.

Bottom line: if you're always paddling at an easy-to-moderate pace in TZ2, you're missing out on training fast-twitch fibres and will race below your potential.

The 2×2 Progression Method

The 2×2 Progression (2x2P) uses two distinctly different progressive interval sessions to increase Functional Threshold Pace (FTP). Each session targets different energy systems — Alactic Power (5–20 sec), Anaerobic Capacity (30–90 sec), Maximal Aerobic Power (3–7 min), and FTP (20 min+). By altering repetitions, duration, and rest intervals across a four-week cycle, every session feels fresh and progression is continuous.

Session 1 — Maximal Aerobic Capacity ("Endurance")

VO₂ intervals: very hard efforts, typically 3–7 minutes duration. Increases aerobic capacity and tolerance to acidosis. One VO₂max session per week, supplemented with longer sub-FTP mileage sessions.

Week 1: TZ1 2.5min / TZ2 2min / TZ3 1.5min / TZ4 1min — Repeat ×8
Week 2: TZ1 2.5min / TZ2 2min / TZ3 1.5min / TZ4 1min / TZ5 0.5min — Repeat ×6
Week 3: TZ1 3min / TZ2 2.5min / TZ3 2min / TZ4 1.5min / TZ5 0.5min — Repeat ×7
Week 4 (adaptation): TZ1 3min / TZ2 2.5min / TZ3 2min / TZ4 1.5min — Repeat ×5

Session 2 — Anaerobic Endurance ("Sprint Endurance")

Maximal efforts for 20–90 seconds. Important for race starts and finishes, and for building tolerance to surging and chasing ocean swells in distance racing.

Week 1: TZ5 0.5min / TZ4 1min / TZ3 1.5min / TZ2 2min / TZ1 2.5min — Repeat ×6
Week 2: TZ5 1min / TZ4 1.5min / TZ3 2min / TZ1 2.5min — Repeat ×7
Week 3: TZ5 1min / TZ4 1.5min / TZ3 2min / TZ2 2.5min / TZ1 3min — Repeat ×6
Week 4: TZ5 45sec / TZ4 1.5min / TZ3 2.5min / TZ1 3min — Repeat ×6

Most paddling races are endurance events, so endurance training is the primary focus. But to maximise performance potential, intervals are essential. The 2×2 Progression builds the metrics to train faster for longer — do these sessions progressively over a four-week cycle and be faster in your next race.

About these metrics. Last active is the last time a member's auth session refreshed — i.e. the last time they had the site open in a tab. The Supabase JS SDK refreshes the JWT roughly hourly while a tab is open and revalidates on every visit, so this catches members who stay signed in and keep browsing without re-authenticating. Threshold is the member's most recent threshold pace, recorded server-side (one row per change). The pill shows how long ago they last updated it — fresh pills = active training, stale pills = drifting. Sessions done counts the sessions they've ticked off on the session page (7d / 30d / all-time). For members who were active before 2026-05-23, completion history is backfilled from their browser's local state on first visit after this update. Sign-ins 30d are a separate signal — fresh authentications (new browser, magic link, password reset) and don't reflect continued use of an existing session.

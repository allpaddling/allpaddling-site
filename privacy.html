Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

All Paddling ("we", "us", "our") is operated by Mick Di Betta, an Australian sole trader. We are committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly when you:

  • Sign up for a training plan membership
  • Complete your onboarding questionnaire (race goals, training hours, ability level)
  • Contact us via email or the contact form
  • Subscribe to our email list

This may include your name, email address, paddle discipline, race goals, and general fitness details. We do not collect sensitive health or medical information beyond what you voluntarily share in your training profile.

2. Payment information

All payments are processed securely by Stripe. We do not store your credit card details on our servers. Stripe's privacy policy governs how your payment information is handled.

3. How we use your information

  • To deliver and personalise your training plan
  • To send transactional emails (payment receipts, plan updates)
  • To respond to your enquiries
  • To send occasional training tips and updates (you can unsubscribe at any time)

4. Disclosure to third parties

We use the following third-party services to operate All Paddling:

  • Supabase — secure database and authentication hosting
  • Stripe — payment processing
  • Resend — transactional email delivery

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any other third parties.

5. Data storage and security

Your data is stored on servers operated by Supabase, which are located in the United States. We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure.

6. Access and correction

You have the right to access the personal information we hold about you and to request corrections. To do so, please contact us at mick@allpaddling.online. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

7. Complaints

If you believe we have not handled your personal information in accordance with the Australian Privacy Principles, you may lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be available at this page. Continued use of our services after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, contact us at mick@allpaddling.online.
